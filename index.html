    UNDER THE HOOD

    AI that shows its work

    The prototype uses transparent browser-based methods so students, analysts, and nontechnical users can understand each decision.

    01 / NLP

    Language processing

    Tokenizes text, recognizes security phrases, and extracts URLs, email addresses, and IP addresses.

    02 / SENTIMENT

    Pressure detection

    Compares emotional and urgency cues to identify fear-based language used in social engineering.

    03 / CLASSIFICATION

    Explainable risk model

    Combines weighted evidence to classify the case and produce a severity-based response plan.

    04 / REINFORCEMENT

    Analyst feedback

    Accepts rewards and corrections, then adjusts risk sensitivity during the active session.

    05 / GENETIC ALGORITHM

    Weight evolution

    Uses selection, crossover, mutation, and fitness scoring to optimize detection weights.
